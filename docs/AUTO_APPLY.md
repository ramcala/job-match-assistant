# Board apply helpers & form autofill

On supported job boards, the extension adds:
- **Load all jobs** (floating box): auto-scrolls to load every listing, then adds buttons to each.
- **Analyze** and **Apply** buttons on each job card.

On an application form (job boards and common ATS — Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, SmartRecruiters,
Ashby, Workable, iCIMS, Breezy, Jobvite), a floating **⚡ Fill application form** button appears. It:
- fills fields it can confidently map from your **Easy Apply saved answers** (Settings) and learned answers,
- **stops rather than guess** on blank required fields it can't map,
- **never clicks Submit** — you review everything and submit yourself,
- offers **Remember my answers** to learn new screening questions for next time.

> Automating application forms can violate a site's terms and risks your account. It runs only on
> your machine, on your click, and never submits for you.
